Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the NewsRiseMedia website and the NewsRiseMedia Publisher application. The application is an internal tool used solely by the NewsRiseMedia account owner to publish our own videos to our own social accounts.

2. Data we collect

The website collects no personal data, sets no tracking cookies, and has no accounts or forms. The Publisher application accesses only the NewsRiseMedia-owned social accounts through each platform’s official API (e.g., the TikTok Content Posting API): it authenticates the account owner, uploads our videos, and reads basic post statistics for those uploads.

3. Third-party user data

We do not collect, process, or store any data about third-party users. No data about anyone other than the account owner is accessed.

4. Storage & security

API tokens for our own accounts are stored locally on operator-controlled hardware, encrypted at the OS level, and are never shared, sold, or transmitted to anyone else. Platform data is retained only as long as needed for posting and performance tracking of our own content.

5. Your rights

If you believe we hold any data about you (e.g., via a platform interaction with our posts), contact us and we will address it in line with applicable law (GDPR/CCPA).
